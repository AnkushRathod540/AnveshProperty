Loan Support

Required Documentation Guide

Reference checklist from the documentation section of your original code.

For Salaried Employees
  • Signed application form along with a photograph
  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, or Passport
  • Address Proof: Passport, utility bills, or Driving License
  • Age Proof: School/College Leaving Certificate or Birth Certificate
  • Income Proof: ITR forms, Form 16, last 6 months salary slips & bank statements
For Self-Employed / Professionals
  • Signed application with 2 photographs
  • ID & Residence Proof such as PAN, Passport, Voter ID, Ration Card or Bank Statement
  • Income: Last 3 years ITRs, computation, P&L and balance sheet
  • Business Proof: Partnership Deed, Registration, MOA & AOA where applicable
  • Bank Statement: Current & SB account for the last 6 months
  • Processing Fees Cheque
  • Copy of Passport, Visa, Service Contract and Salary Slips (attested)
  • GPA (General Power of Attorney) in prescribed format
  • Overseas bank statements and NRI account statements as applicable
  • Salary documents should be submitted in English
MHADA / PCNTDA / MIDC / MHB Plots
  • Allotment letter & NOC from the concerned body
  • Lease deed & registration receipts
  • Sanctioned building plan & commencement certificate
Loan Take-Over
  • Outstanding loan quotation & repayment track record
  • List of documents & NOC for take-over from current bank
  • Copies of property agreement & sanction plan
  • Agreement to sale with the builder & registration receipts
  • Blueprint (attested) with the flat marked as required
  • Payment receipts to builder
  • NOC from builder & Builder Demand Letter
Important: The income documents may also be required for the co-applicant if their income is being considered for the loan.
